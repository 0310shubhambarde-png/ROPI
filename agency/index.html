How it runs

Three steps. No new process for your team.

Step 1

Send the domain and three pages

You tell me the practice, the pages, and the appointment route it uses today. I reply with a fixed price and a confirmed delivery date.

Step 2

Send the assets

Copy, logo, images, CMS or hosting access, and the scheduler link. The delivery clock starts when the last item arrives, and that date is confirmed in writing.

Step 3

Approve one staging link

You review the staged pages and request one agreed correction pass. Final payment is due on approval, before launch. Source files hand over with it.

White-label terms

Built so your client never meets me.

  • Your branding. Delivered under your agency name, or unbranded for you to rebrand. No footer credit unless you approve one.
  • No client contact. Every message runs through your contact. I do not email, call, or message the practice.
  • Non-solicit. I will not approach the practice for any work, at any time, for any reason.
  • NDA on request. Signed before any assets or account details are shared.
  • Source files on final payment. Nothing is held back.
  • No patient data, ever. No intake forms, no EHR connections, no clinical content decisions. The practice approves all clinical and scheduling wording.
